# SiC MOSFET shortlist for the 10 kW DAB (800 V / 400 V, 100 kHz) — datasheet-sourced

Collected 2026-10-21 by the AIPE research step. Every datasheet below was
downloaded from the manufacturer's own server and text-extracted; values are
transcribed as printed, nothing is estimated. "Hot" RDS(on) is the datasheet's
own high-Tj row. Where a page was client-rendered or blocked, that is stated.
These are **manufacturer-specified** values (Core evidence kind `source`,
source type `datasheet`), not AIPE measurements. Candidate records derived from
this sheet must keep the datasheet URL, revision and test conditions.

Primary bridge (800 V bus) needs 1200 V class devices; secondary bridge (400 V
bus) accepts 650 V, 700 V, 750 V or 900 V class devices.

## Model access and licensing notes

- **Wolfspeed** — [LTspice and PLECS models page](https://www.wolfspeed.com/tools-and-support/power/ltspice-and-plecs-models/): downloading means agreeing to the Terms of Use; no login stated. [Terms of Use](https://www.wolfspeed.com/legal/terms-of-use/) permit personal, non-commercial download/display/print and require written consent for other copying, distribution or publication — a redistribution restriction. Module SPICE and RC-thermal models are documented in PRD-07913 and PRD-09035.
- **Infineon** — product pages are client-rendered; PLECS models are listed for the three discretes; AN_2020_17 states SPICE .lib files work in SIMetrix/LTspice/PSpice/OrCAD and PLECS models are XML. Per-part file, licence text and login status not verified; gated documents need a myInfineon account; a community reply says some SiC module SPICE models are NDA-only.
- **onsemi** — Elite Power Simulator and the PLECS Model Generator require MyON login. SPICE ZIPs are published per AND90315 on product pages; login requirement and licence text not confirmed.
- **Microchip** — models are provided after a [licence agreement form](https://www.microchip.com/en-us/products/power-management/silicon-carbide-sic-devices-and-power-modules/design-resources/sic-spice-and-plecs-component-models-license-agreement); automated fetch blocked (HTTP 403), licence wording not read. Microchip support states it cannot support LTspice due to Analog Devices restrictions.
- **ROHM** — [Design Models page](https://www.rohm.com/support/design-model): SPICE/LTspice and PLECS/PSIM thermal models downloadable from product pages; unencrypted SPICE on request; no login stated. PSIM models confirmed for SCT4036KR and SCT4045DR.
- **ST** — SCT040W120G3-4 lists a SPICE model v1.0 (24 Aug 2023); SCT040W65G3-4 lists none. Licence wording not seen.

## Data gaps

- FF45MR12W1M1_B11 datasheet not retrieved; no Microchip module (site blocked).
- Eon/Eoff absent from the IMZA65R027M1H tables; Infineon G2 and the onsemi module give Qfr, not Qrr.
- Only onsemi manufacturer reference prices were seen; no distributor price for any part.
- Switching-energy test conditions differ between manufacturers (voltage, current, RG, Tj, freewheeling device), so Eon/Eoff values are not directly comparable without scaling to the DAB operating point; this is one reason the AIPE workflow issues double-pulse measurement requests.
